# Parent Pass

Your pass into the games your kids play.

Clear, practical guides for parents to Roblox, Brawl Stars, Minecraft and Fortnite: what children actually do in each game, what's popular right now, the real risks, and step-by-step parental controls.

- The whole site is one file: `index.html`.
- "Right now" notes are checked weekly; parental-control steps and age ratings are fact-checked monthly against official sources.
